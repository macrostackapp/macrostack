# Contributors

People who sent focus stacks to test and improve the stacking engine. Everyone who helps is credited in
the app and on the project page, under the name they chose, unless they asked to stay anonymous. Their
images are used only for testing: never published or shared without their permission, and deleted on
request.

When stacks arrive, add one row per contributor and register each stack in `tools/bench/sets.txt` (its
"what it tests" column can mention the contributor).
